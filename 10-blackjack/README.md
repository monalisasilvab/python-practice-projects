# Blackjack

Simple command-line Blackjack game. Deals cards to player and computer (dealer), lets the player hit or stand, and determines the winner.

## Rules implemented
- Aces count as 11 or 1.
- A two-card 21 is treated as Blackjack.
- Dealer draws until their score is at least 17.
- Game compares scores and prints outcome: win, lose, draw, or Blackjack results.
